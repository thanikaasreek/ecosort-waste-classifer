How it works

From photo to disposal advice in four steps.

1

Upload a photo

Take a picture or upload an image of the waste item.

2

Preprocessing

The image is resized to 224×224 and normalised for the model.

3

CNN prediction

A MobileNetV2 transfer-learning model predicts the waste category.

4

Disposal tip

You get the category, confidence score and how to dispose of it.

Waste guide

Know which bin each type of waste belongs in.

Dry waste

Plastic

Rinse and place in the dry waste bin. Avoid single-use items.

Dry waste

Paper & cardboard

Keep dry and flatten boxes before recycling.

Dry waste

Glass

Handle carefully and give to a recycler or collector.

Dry waste

Metal

Rinse cans and sell to scrap dealers or recycle.

Wet waste

Organic / biological

Compost at home or use the green wet-waste bin.

Hazardous

Batteries & e-waste

Never bin them. Use authorised e-waste collection points.

About the project

Improper segregation of waste is a major problem for cities. This project uses computer vision to automate waste identification and promote correct recycling.

Tech stack

Python, TensorFlow/Keras, MobileNetV2, Flask, HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Dataset

Waste images across multiple classes, with augmentation (flip, rotation, zoom).
